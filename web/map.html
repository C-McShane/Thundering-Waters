Hazard site points▾
Every documented contaminated site in Niagara County, each dot coloured by its regulatory programme. Turn the layer on or off, or show/hide individual programme types below — the list doubles as the colour legend.
Filter by site type▾
Show or hide sites by regulatory programme. The colour swatches are also the map's colour key.
Area layers▾
Shaded areas rather than points. Contamination by tract shades each census tract by the share of its land that is contaminated; the impact zone outlines the Niagara Falls area where most sites cluster. Each legend appears automatically when you switch its layer on.

Hazard Sites▾
Documented radioactive/radiological sites, by the material they contain — isotopes (U/Th/Ra) and class (TENORM slag, FUSRAP Manhattan-Project legacy, MED-AEC weapons-era facilities).
Select radioactive material▾
Select a material to show the sites that contain it.
Monitoring Wells & Soil▾
Wells and soil zones with measured radionuclides, grouped by element. Total Uranium & Radium-226 have multi-year trends; the isotopes are mostly single-year snapshots; Mill No. 2 soil is a one-time gamma survey (no time series). Click any point for its plot.
Select radionuclide▾
Select a radionuclide to map the wells & soil that carry it.
Radioactive Soil Zones▾
Delineated soil / slag zones where a gamma survey found radioactive material (uranium, thorium, radium) — a footprint, not a groundwater plume. Toggle a site to map its zones. More sites will appear here as they're added.
Filter by contaminant across the hazard sites and the monitoring-well network. Wells carry sampling dates — their menu adds a year filter, per-well time-series plots, and a running total by year.
Hazard Sites▾
Sites that list a contaminant in their NYSDEC record. These are chemical presence flags — sites don't carry sampling dates, so there's no year filter or trend on this side.
Shows sites that list the selected contaminant. Reflects recorded chemicals (107 of 256 sites list any).
Monitoring Wells & Soil▾
Wells with measured chemistry. Pick a chemical to map where it was detected (the well layers switch on automatically); add a year to show detections by that year or earlier. Click a well for its concentration-vs-year plot; the running total appears bottom-left.
Shows wells where the selected contaminant was detected.
Pick a year to see which wells had a chemical detected by that year or earlier.
Well layers▾
Each water droplet is a well or sampling station — a blood-red core (contamination), sized by the number of chemicals detected, with a ring colour showing what it samples (see “Filter by sampling type”). The three toggles choose the data source; legacy wells' popups carry a ⚑ approximate-location note.
Filter by Sampling Type▾
With nothing ticked, every well shows as a blood-red drop with a grey outline. Tick one or more types — bedrock vs. overburden groundwater, surface water, remediation/recovery — to show only those wells (the rest hide), their outlines taking the type colour. The swatches are the colour key.
Site Specific▾
Per-site views for sites that report data outside the standard chemical dropdown. Each site is its own drop-down; more will be added here.
S-Area · Intensity Over Time▾
S-Area's 184 wells report Total Organic Concentration, not named chemicals. Pick a year to colour the wells by the latest TOC reading known by that year.
Love Canal▾
Everything for Love Canal in one place — the monitoring wells (contaminant chemistry), the piezometers (water levels), and the leachate-containment infrastructure.
Wells carry contaminant chemistry (click one for its concentration-vs-year plot). Piezometers measure groundwater levels (2015–2022). Pumps & tanks are the containment collection infrastructure.
