Water-Testing & Monitoring Wells
Each water droplet is a well or sampling station — a blood-red core (the contamination), sized by the number of chemicals detected. Its ring colour shows what it samples (see the sampling-type legend below). The three toggles above choose the data source — USGS/EPA Water Quality Portal, NYSDEC cleanup-report wells, or legacy hazardous/radioactive sites. Each well's source and (for legacy wells) its ⚑ approximate-location note are shown in its popup.
Filter by Sampling Type
Ring colour = what the point samples. Show or hide by type — bedrock vs. overburden groundwater, surface water, or remediation/recovery wells — across all sources at once.
Filter Wells by Chemical
...by Year
Shows wells where the selected contaminant was detected. Turn a well layer on above to see results.
Pick a year to see which wells had a chemical detected by that year or earlier — this is how a contaminant's spread shows up: more wells lighting up as the year advances. Works best combined with a chemical above; with no chemical picked it shows wells with any tracked contaminant detected by that year.
S-Area: Contamination Intensity Over Time
S-Area's 184 wells report Total Organic Concentration (NAPL-era chemistry), not named chemicals, so they get their own year view. Pick a year to color wells by the latest TOC reading known by that year.
