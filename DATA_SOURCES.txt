NOTES ON BLOCK GROUPS CONTAMINATION LAYER
================================================================================

The block_groups_contamination layer (175 census block groups) was updated
on 2026-06-07 by:
  1. Spatial join of nysdec_all_sites (points) to block group polygons (within)
  2. Aggregating area_acres_best per block group (site_count, total_acres)
  3. Recalculating pct_contaminated = total_acres / aland_acres * 100
  4. Writing updated acres_from_polygons = total_acres

Note: pct_contaminated can exceed 100% where multiple overlapping sites
are summed within a single block group (acreages are non-exclusive).
All 223 nysdec_all_sites points were successfully matched to block groups.

2. BLOCK GROUPS LAYER — METRIC REDESIGN
   Problem identified: 2 block groups showed pct_contaminated > 100% (max 159.4%).
   Root cause: pct_contaminated was calculated as sum(site_acres) / aland_acres,
   but site acreages are self-reported footprints — when multiple large adjacent
   or overlapping sites are both assigned to the same block group, their reported
   acreages sum above the block group's total land area. This is methodologically
   honest but not publishable.

   Resolution — three new fields replace pct_contaminated:
     a) coverage_pct: = min(total_acres / aland_acres * 100, 100.0)
        Capped at 100%. Suitable for choropleth mapping. Maximum value: 100.0%.
        Preserves the underlying total_acres field so readers can see the raw sum.
     b) overlap_flag: boolean. True where sum of site acres exceeds aland_acres.
        Flags the 2 block groups where stacking of large industrial sites produces
        sums exceeding land area. Allows map labeling: "multiple overlapping sites".
     c) sites_per_sqmi: = site_count / (aland_acres / 640)
        Site density per square mile. Never exceeds a ceiling, interpretable at
        any geographic scale. Maximum value in dataset: 28.3 sites/sqmi.
   pct_contaminated field removed from layer.

KEY FIELD DEFINITIONS:
  observed_[cancer]: Actual diagnosed cases in the block group, 2011–2015
  expected_[cancer]: Expected cases if NYS age-adjusted rate applied to local population
  SIR (Standardized Incidence Ratio): observed / expected
    SIR = 1.0 → exactly what NYS demographics would predict
    SIR > 1.0 → more cases than expected; SIR = 2.0 → twice as many
  highlight_[cancer]: NYSDOH's own statistical significance flag
    +1 = statistically significantly ELEVATED (not due to chance)
     0 = not statistically significant
    -1 = statistically significantly LOW
  NOTE: The SIR benchmark is New York State, not the US national rate.
  NYS itself runs elevated vs. the US national rate for many industrial
  cancers due to its manufacturing history. National comparisons therefore
  compound the elevation shown in the NYS-benchmarked SIR values.

    - Approximate locations: many legacy wells carry a coord_precision flag (site-centroid,
      map-relative, or approximate). Each is disclosed in its own popup.
    - Chemical presence flags on hazard sites are RECORDED chemicals from the NYSDEC record,
      not measurements. 106 of 255 sites list any curated chemical; a larger 121 carry raw
      NYSDEC free-text. These two populations are reported separately in statistics.json
      and must not be conflated.
    - Cancer data are ecological (population-level). They do not establish individual
      exposure, disease causation, or a causal relationship with mapped sites.
    - An ecological regression of cancer SIR against hazard-site density was run on
      2026-07-19 and deliberately NOT published: the only signal (lung) was confounded by
      smoking/SES which the data cannot control, and bladder — the a-priori industrial
      cancer — was null. Inputs and outputs retained in csv/ for transparency.
    - Selected high detections are pending full source citations; see docs/CITATIONS_TODO.md.
