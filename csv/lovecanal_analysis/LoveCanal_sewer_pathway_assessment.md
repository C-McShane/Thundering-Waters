# Sewer-system pathway assessment — "fast sewer vs. old contamination" tension

Prepared 2026-07-17. Raised by the user: if the sewer is fast-moving, pre-1978
contamination should have flushed through long ago — so "old" NAPL in the Colvin
sewer in 2011 seems physically odd. This is a sharp and correct question. Resolution:

## Two different "sewer" compartments (this resolves the tension)
1. **Pipe interior (flowing wastewater): FAST.** Dissolved contaminants entering the
   live sewer flush to the treatment plant in hours-days. The user is RIGHT — old
   *dissolved-phase* contamination in the flowing pipe would be long gone.
2. **Bedding / trench backfill (granular gravel-sand around the pipe): SLOW + PERSISTENT.**
   Not flowing. More permeable than the surrounding native clay. The 2011 contamination
   was found HERE (the bedding), not in the flowing pipe. NAPL pools and persists here.

## Why NAPL persists for decades (the key)
The Love Canal organics (chlorobenzene, chlorinated benzenes/solvents) form **DNAPL**
(dense non-aqueous phase liquid — denser than water, immiscible). DNAPL sinks, pools,
and is held immobile in pore spaces by capillary forces. It is the textbook *persistent
source*: a stranded DNAPL residual can sit 50+ years slowly dissolving, without flowing
away. So "old NAPL in the bedding" is physically coherent even decades later — the
flush-through logic applies to the dissolved phase, NOT to stranded DNAPL.

## The trench as a PATHWAY (relevant to the whole migration thesis)
The permeable sewer bedding is also a **preferential conduit** — groundwater and DNAPL
move ALONG the trench faster than through native clay. Historically this is THE Love
Canal off-site migration mechanism (1970s spread went via storm sewers/swales). So the
sanitary-sewer trench network is a fast "highway" potentially connecting the canal to
distant residential points (Colvin, 99th St).

## The real open question (what to actually assess)
Is the Colvin NAPL:
- (a) a FINITE old stranded slug (persisting but not replenished), or
- (b) CONTINUOUSLY FED from an upgradient source (the canal) via the trench pathway?

Evidence bearing on it:
- FOR (a): after the Feb 2011 excavation, MW-3 (set in the repaired bedding) saw NO
  NAPL return 2011-2018. If continuously fed, NAPL should have recurred. DEC concluded
  "not a result of current operations." Nearby groundwater wells show no spike in 2012
  (this analysis) or 2015-2019. => at the repaired 96th-97th segment, looks finite/removed.
- CAVEAT (critical): MW-3 monitors ONE point (the 96th-97th segment). The trench extends
  EAST toward 99th St / the pool digger (1026 99th, ~290 m ENE), and that eastward extent
  was NOT characterized. A separate stranded DNAPL pocket could persist east toward the
  pool digger, independent of MW-3's clean record.

## On "predates the Love Canal facility"
"Predates the facility" (pre-1942 dumping) is likely journalistic imprecision. The
physically coherent reading: legacy Love Canal DNAPL (1940s-50s waste) that migrated into
the sewer trench PRE-REMEDIATION (pre-1978) and persists as stranded, immobile residual.
Get the PRIMARY DEC determination for exact wording before citing.
