*** CORRECTION NOTICE (2026-07-17) *********************************************
A medium-mislabeling bug was found and fixed AFTER the first cut. The suffix
letter in the 2015 report ("A/B/C/D Wells") is NOT a geologic-medium code; it
varies by well cluster. The original code assumed A=Silty Sand/Fill, which is
false — at strings 1180/1190 the "A" wells are DEEP Glacial Till.

Consequence: the original headline "string 1190 is a SHALLOW containment weak
link (marginal/outward shallow gradient, 3 reversals)" is RETRACTED. Those were
deep Glacial Till readings mislabeled as shallow. See KEY FINDINGS (revised) and
LoveCanal_VULNERABILITIES.md #1. The medium is now assigned per well from the
authoritative 2016-2022 headers (WELL_MEDIUM).

QUESTION
--------
Niagara County had several high-precipitation years in the piezometer window
(2015-2022). Precipitation infiltrating the Love Canal cap/soils generates
leachate that the barrier-drain pumps must remove to keep an INWARD hydraulic
gradient (inside water level held below outside → groundwater flows toward the
drain → contaminants contained). If the pumps + storage + treatment can't keep
up, the inside level rises, the inward gradient weakens or reverses, and
leachate can migrate outward. This analysis looks for that signature.

DATA SOURCES (all from the site operator's own NYSDEC filings)
-------------------------------------------------------------
1. Pump capacity — 2013 O&M Manual (docs/LoveCanal_932020_2013_OM_Manual.pdf, p18):
   - Northern/Central: PC-1A, PC-2A, PC-3A — two Gorman-Rupp S2B65-E2 pumps EACH,
     rated 50 GPM @ 35 ft TDH (6 pumps).
   - Southern: PC-1 & PC-2 — one pump EACH rated 100 GPM @ 40 ft TDH; PC-3 — two
     pumps rated 50 GPM @ 50 ft TDH.
   - ~600 GPM total collection capacity; holding-tank buffer ~50,850 gal
     (25,850 N/C + 25,000 S). Pumps run as-needed, individually or together.
2. Pump LOAD + PRECIPITATION — Table 3.1 "Monthly Volumes of Groundwater Treated"
   (Love Canal PRRs). Monthly Gross/Net gallons + discharge-days, and annual
   precipitation (NCDC, Niagara Falls Int'l Airport). Volumes 2000-2019 parsed
   from the 2019 PRR; annual precipitation 2000-2021 (2022 PRR).
3. WATER LEVELS — Tables 3.6A-F ("<string> Series Piezometers Water Levels"),
   all five PRRs (2015, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2022). Quarterly, ft above mean sea
   level, per nested piezometer, grouped by geologic medium. The report orders
   each medium block "most-distant OUTSIDE the tile drain → tile drain → INSIDE."

METHOD
------
* Operating pump rate per month = Net gallons / (discharge-days × 1440 min) = GPM
  while the plant ran. Annual average rate = Net / 525,600 min.
* Inward gradient per (piezometer string × geologic medium × date) =
  mean(outside-drain wells) − mean(inside-drain wells), classified by each well's
  x-position relative to the "Tile Drain" column in the source table. Positive =
  inward (contained); ≤ 0 = outward (potential breach).
* MEDIUM MATTERS: the barrier drain (12-25 ft deep) intercepts the SHALLOW
  overburden, so "Silty Sand/Fill" (the water-table / canal-fill unit that
  carries leachate) is the containment-relevant signal. A higher INSIDE head in
  the DEEP "Glacial Till" is most likely natural confined-aquifer head, NOT a
  containment breach — the two are reported separately and must not be conflated.
* Spatial: each string's centroid (from the hand-collected piezometer coords) →
  distance & bearing to the reported 99th St / Black Creek Village leachate
  encounter (43.0932, -78.9487).

KEY FINDINGS
------------
1. Pump LOAD tracks precipitation, as expected. Wettest year 2017 (41.4 in) →
   highest load: 5.77M gal net, avg 11.0 GPM, peak month (April) ~30 GPM, 25
   discharge-days. Dry 2015/2016 (~24 in) → ~5-6 GPM avg.
2. BUT even the wettest year's PEAK MONTHLY rate (~30 GPM) stayed well under the
   ~50 GPM per-pump rating and far under the ~600 GPM system capacity, with a
   ~50k-gal storage buffer. At monthly resolution there is NO evidence the pumps
   were overwhelmed.
3. The OVERALL inward gradient is STABLE across years, NOT weakest in wet years.
   (An earlier quick cut suggested temporal weakening; artifact of not classifying
   inside vs outside per medium block. Corrected.)
4. [REVISED after the medium-label fix — see lc_gradient_summary_CORRECTED.csv]
   - SHALLOW (Silty Sand/Fill), the containment-relevant water-table unit: the
     inside/outside well-pair method DOES NOT APPLY here. All 6 shallow wells are
     OUTBOARD sentinels (verified 100% outside, never inside, every year); none is
     screened inside the drain ring — as expected, since inside the ring is buried
     waste. So mean(out)-mean(in) can't be formed for the shallow unit at any
     string (least of all 1170/1190 = no shallow well, 1180 = one). This is a
     METHOD-APPLICABILITY limit, NOT a monitoring gap/negligence and NOT evidence
     of a breach. What shallow data exists is CONTAINMENT-CONSISTENT: outboard
     shallow heads sit 6-13 ft ABOVE the drain invert (the fixed "Tile Drain"
     datum, constant per string: 1190=554.8, 1180=560.0 ft), i.e. the water table
     slopes down toward the lower drain.
   - INTERMEDIATE (Soft Clay / Fractured Clay) and DEEP (Glacial Till): almost all
     strongly INWARD (+2 to +5 ft, 0 reversals). The lone exception is string
     1190's DEEP Glacial Till (mean -0.43 ft, 11/20 reversals) — a single inside
     well (1190A) reading above the outboard till wells. That is a basal
     confined-aquifer head relationship, hydraulically separate from the shallow
     leachate zone; it is NOT a shallow-containment metric regardless of cause.
   - The confirmed 99th-St contamination ("pool digger, residential side of 99th
     between Colvin and Moschel", ~43.0867,-78.9482) is nearest string 1180
     (~230 m), which HOLDS inward in every computable unit. The 1190 deep-till
     anomaly is ~560 m south and NOT co-located with the leak.

INTERPRETATION (measured, revised)
----------------------------------
The pumps do NOT appear to have been broadly overwhelmed by wet years at the
resolution available — capacity headroom is large and the computable
(intermediate/deep) gradients held. The earlier "string 1190 shallow weak-link"
is WITHDRAWN: it was a medium-labeling artifact, and 1190 has no shallow
instrumentation at all. The honest, defensible position: the piezometer
inward-gradient method does not apply to the SHALLOW unit on the 99th-St flank
(no inside-drain shallow well by design), so it can neither confirm nor refute
shallow containment there — but the shallow heads that exist (above the drain
invert) and every paired deeper-unit gradient (+2 to +5 ft inward) are
containment-consistent. The only residual data feature — 1190 deep Glacial Till —
is a confined-head condition needing hydrogeologic review, not a demonstrated
barrier breach. Event-scale (storm-peak) testing is moot for the shallow unit
here given the above; no sub-quarterly water-level data exists in the PRR bodies
regardless (see CAVEATS + VULNERABILITIES #6).

CAVEATS / LIMITATIONS
---------------------
- Water levels are QUARTERLY and volumes MONTHLY; neither resolves a short
  storm-peak event when a transient overwhelm would actually occur.
- Shallow (Silty Sand/Fill) is sparsely tabulated (~20 readings total), so
  per-string/per-year precip correlation for the shallow unit is under-powered.
- "Inside" often reduces to a single well (n_in=1) per string/medium — the
  gradient is sensitive to that one measurement.
- The deep Glacial Till reversals need hydrogeologic confirmation (natural vs
  breach) before any claim.
- 2022 gradient year lacks a matched precipitation figure (precip table ends
  2021); 2000-2019 monthly VOLUMES only (2022-PRR volume table = 22 tight
  columns, resisted position-binning; precipitation recovered positionally).
- Straight-line distance to the leak is muddied because the reported point is
  ~1.3 km north of all piezometers; bearing is the more meaningful spatial cue.
