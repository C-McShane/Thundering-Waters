# Old-pulse / genuine-increase test on the merged 2000–2019 Love Canal record

Prepared 2026-07-17. Merged series `csv/lovecanal_analysis/lc_chem_2000_2019_MERGED.csv`
= OCC Table 3.3 (2000,2001,2005,2006,2009) + PRR Table 3.2 (2010–2014) + ConcSeries
(2015–2019). Years present: 2000,2001,2005,2006,2009,2010,2011,2012,2013,2014,2015,
2016,2017,2018,2019 (15 yrs). GAPS: 2002,2003,2004,2007,2008 (OCC Table 3.3 scanned or
parse-failed those years) + 2013/2018 partial. 1990–1999 NOT obtained: the OCC
"Table 3.4 (1990-to-year)" multi-year table only tracks ONE selected well (10210A, a
low-contamination bedrock sentinel), NOT the 10135 hotspot.

## Headline: the hotspot is a STABLE persistent source, not a receding pulse
MW-10135 detected concentrations, 2000–2019:
- Chlorobenzene 2300,2700,2000,2400,1300,1100,2500,2300,2600,2700,2600,2400 — flat.
- Benzene 6400,7600,6800,3400,2200,5900,6100,6200,7100,6300,5700 — flat.
- Toluene 21000,22000,16000,21000,11000,3100,14000,20000,20000,23000,19000,19000 — flat.
- (A synchronized dip across all analytes in 2010–2011 then full recovery — most likely
  a low-flow/dilution sampling artifact, not a trend.)
=> 20 years of stability. This argues AGAINST "an old pulse that crested and is now
receding" (which predicts monotonic decline) and FOR a persistent DNAPL source, exactly
as EPA characterizes 10135 (isolated, stable). Since 10135 is flat from 2000 on, it was
most plausibly a similar stable source in the 1990s too — not a decaying pulse.

## Old-pulse (declining) scan across all wells
NO well shows a clean early-high/later-low receding-pulse pattern, except:
- 10135 Vinyl chloride 110 (2000) -> 4 (2019). But vinyl chloride is a DEGRADATION
  product (reductive dechlorination of TCE/PCE/DCE); its decline while parent compounds
  stay flat reflects changing redox, not a receding source. Ambiguous, not supportive.

## Genuine-increase scan
Mostly single-year high readings at 10135 in 2001 (TCE 60->140, PCE 16->50, DCB 22->48,
1,1,2,2-TeCA 27->100) that came back down — a high-concentration sampling year, not a
sustained rise. Plus the known artifacts (10135 chloroform 100->180 2015).
ONE genuine flag worth follow-up: **PCM-09 (102nd St) Arsenic 10 -> 50 µg/L in 2018**
(5x, above guidance). BUT arsenic is redox-mobilized (often natural), not a Love Canal
organic — likely geochemical, not migration. Flag for a look, don't over-read.

## Verdict on the old-pulse hypothesis
The 20-year groundwater record does NOT show a contaminant pulse that crested and
receded anywhere in the monitored network. The mass is a stable persistent source
(10135, south) plus the sewer-bedding stranded NAPL (Colvin) — the latter still
consistent with "old but stranded DNAPL," which does not require a moving/decaying
groundwater plume. So: old-pulse-via-groundwater = not supported; old-stranded-DNAPL-
in-sewer-bedding = still viable (and is a reservoir, not a pulse).
CAVEAT: monitored wells only; the pool-digger point itself is unmonitored. And 1990s
10135 data would strengthen this — obtainable only from a 1990s RI report or the
scanned Table 3.4 in later OCC reports (OCR).
