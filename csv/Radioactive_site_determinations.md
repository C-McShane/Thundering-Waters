# Radioactive-site tagging — determinations (2026-07-18)

For the Radiation tab, every candidate site was verified against authoritative records
(US DOL **EEOICPA** covered-facility list, Manhattan Engineer District / AEC contractor
history, NYSDEC site records) rather than tagged by name. Sources below.

## ADDED — documented AEC/MED radioactive-material facilities (`rad_class = "MED-AEC"`)
- **Carborundum Company, Globar Plant [932036]** (3425 Hyde Park Blvd) — fabricated uranium
  rods for the MED (1943–44); made uranium, plutonium and carbide pellets + zirconium/
  hafnium/titanium for AEC reactor-materials programs (1959–67). EEOICPA AWE/DOE facility.
  rad_iso U, Th.
- **Carborundum — South of Building 89 [932048A]** (1801 Buffalo Ave) — a Buffalo Avenue
  location of the MED uranium-rod forming/coating/canning work. rad_iso U.
- **Carborundum Corp, Bldg 82/30/32 [932048B]** (Buffalo Ave) — part of the Buffalo Avenue
  plant complex that performed MED uranium work. rad_iso U.

Basis: EEOICPA lists "Carborundum Company, Niagara Falls" as an AWE/DOE covered facility for
its **Globar Plant and Buffalo Avenue locations**; the two Buffalo-Ave DEC parcels and the
Globar parcel are those locations.

## EXCLUDED — verified NOT radioactive (no documented basis)
- **Vanadium Corp of America [932001]** + **Airco Properties [932001B]** (4400 Lockport /
  4201 Witmer) — vanadium/ferroalloy smelting; **not** on the EEOICPA radioactive list, no
  AEC nuclear role found. (My initial hunch that VCA was radioactive did not survive
  verification.)
- **Airco Speer Carbon-Graphite [932002]** (Packard Rd @ 47th St) — graphite electrode
  graphitizing; supplied graphite flour but no uranium processing; not EEOICPA-listed.
- **Carborundum — Abrasive Division [932007]** (6600 Walmore Rd, Wheatfield) and
  **Carborundum Specialty Products [932102]** (2050 Cory Dr, Sanborn) — abrasives/ceramics
  at separate suburban sites, NOT the Globar/Buffalo-Ave AEC-work locations.
- **GrafTech Intl (formerly Union Carbide) [932035]** (Hyde Park Blvd) — Union Carbide's
  **carbon/graphite electrode** plant (later UCAR/GrafTech), a different division from the
  uranium-metal **Electro Metallurgical** plant; not on the radioactive list.
- **Union Carbide Site [B00079]** (3625 Highland Ave) — no evidence it is the Electromet
  uranium plant or otherwise radioactive.
- **Stauffer Chemical — Whittaker [932034], Art Park [932049], PASNY [932053], PASNY Upper
  Mountain [932091B]** — chemical plants; the thorium contamination reported near Artpark is
  **regional NFSS/LOOW-legacy** material (DEC aerial-survey findings), not Stauffer-sourced;
  not on the EEOICPA radioactive list.

## Note on Electro Metallurgical (the confirmed MED uranium-metal plant, 47th St)
Electromet — "the largest ore-to-metal uranium production plant for the Manhattan Project,
1942–53" (reduced UF4 to uranium metal) — sits in the 47th St corridor already represented by
the tagged **Northern Ethanol Redevelopment Site [C932143]** (137 47th St, rad_class TENORM).

## Caveat
`rad_class = "MED-AEC"` records document **historical** radioactive-material handling
(EEOICPA worker-exposure basis); it is not a statement that the parcel is a designated
FUSRAP site or has quantified residual contamination today. The popup states this basis.
