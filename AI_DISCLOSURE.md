# AI Assistance and Human Responsibility

*Thundering Waters* is an independent, human-directed project developed with substantial assistance from Anthropic's Claude.

Caitlin McShane conceived and directed the project, assembled its core public-record sources, established the research questions, developed the spatial compilation, and made the final editorial and publication decisions. The project also builds upon Christen Civiletto's original compilation of 89 Niagara County sites.

Claude was used extensively as a technical assistant. Its contributions included generating and revising portions of the Python, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS; converting existing data into formats used by the web application; assisting with PDF-table extraction and georeferencing workflows; organizing documentation and version-control tasks; and assisting with exploratory analyses at the author's direction. Exploratory analyses that did not receive sufficient validation were not used to support the map's published conclusions.

AI-generated and AI-modified work is not treated as an independent authority. The author remains responsible for publication decisions, validation against primary sources, documentation of uncertainty, and correction of errors. Some portions of the code, extracted records, and georeferenced locations remain under human review, and independent technical review is being sought. Known unverified items and open audit issues are identified in the interface or project documentation.

The map should be used as a documentary index and exploratory visualization of public records—not as a substitute for the underlying regulatory documents or as proof of exposure, individual health risk, or environmental causation. Where this project differs from a cited primary source, the primary source governs.

Known limitations, validation progress, methodology changes, and corrections are documented separately in the repository ([`VALIDATION_STATUS.md`](VALIDATION_STATUS.md), [`CORRECTIONS.md`](CORRECTIONS.md), and [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md)). Corrections are welcomed through the project's [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/C-McShane/Thundering-Waters/issues/new).

This project was undertaken independently. It does not represent the work, conclusions, or views of the University of Louisville or the University of Colorado Boulder. Neither institution has reviewed the project's technical or scientific content or endorsed its conclusions.
