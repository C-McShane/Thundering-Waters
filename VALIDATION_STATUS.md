# Validation status

What has and has not been checked, as of 2026-07-20. This project is published iteratively: some
material is source-verified, some is under review, and the parts that are not yet verified are
labelled as such — both here and in the interface itself.

Three levels are used below:

- **Source-verified** — checked against the primary regulatory document or agency dataset.
- **Under review** — in the map and documented, but not yet independently confirmed against source.
- **Pending** — known to be incomplete; flagged in the interface.

## Georeferenced positions

- **480 monitoring points carry an approximate position** and disclose it in their own popup. The
  full inventory, grouped by site and method, is in [`docs/APPROXIMATE_LOCATIONS.md`](docs/APPROXIMATE_LOCATIONS.md).
- Placement methods and their stated accuracy (e.g. the Covanta geoprobe fit, mean nearest-neighbour
  error 4.6 m) are documented there.
- **These placements are under review** and have not all been independently confirmed against the
  source figures. Human validation material — including placement-check images — is in
  [`validation/`](validation/).

## Selected high detections

- The "Selected high detections" shown in the Chemicals and Radiation tabs are **pending full source
  citations**. Each of the 8 required fields (report title, table, page, sample medium, sampling
  date, comparison standard, source link, selection criterion) is tracked in
  [`docs/CITATIONS_TODO.md`](docs/CITATIONS_TODO.md).
- **112 citation fields across 14 entries remain to be filled.** Any entry with incomplete citations
  renders a visible **pending** marker in the interface — a pending marker means the figure is not
  yet fully sourced, not that it is disputed.
- The comparison standards themselves (federal MCL vs NYSDEC AWQS for groundwater; NYSDEC Part 375
  SCO tier for soil) are still to be named and are noted as an open decision in the citations file.

## Counts and figures shown in the interface

- Every count displayed by the map is generated from the data by `web/build_statistics.py` and read
  from `web/data/statistics.json`. No count is typed by hand, in the interface or the README.
- Recency figures (`last_sampled`, 1948–2025) are generated by `web/build_recency.py`.

## Hand validation in progress

The author is working through independent validation. Working material is in [`validation/`](validation/),
including the sample-point checklist, the radiological-candidate list, and placement-check images.

## How to report a problem

Corrections are welcomed through the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/C-McShane/Thundering-Waters/issues/new).
Where this project differs from a cited primary source, the primary source governs.
